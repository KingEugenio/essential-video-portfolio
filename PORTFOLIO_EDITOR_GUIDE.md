# Video Editor Portfolio Platform — User & Editor Guide

Welcome to your **Retro Digital Surrealism Video Editor Portfolio Platform**. This guide explains how to manage your projects, upload or link videos from Google Drive, customize your brand aesthetics, and receive client inquiries.

---

## 1. Quick Start & Private Admin Gateway

Your public portfolio website is completely clean and visitor-facing. No editing buttons or admin tools are ever exposed to your public visitors.

- **Access Private Admin**: Append `/admin` to your website URL (e.g. `https://your-portfolio.com/admin` or click the hidden shortcut).
- **Master Password**: Default password is `admin123` or `ekopixels2026`. You can change this in your server environment settings.
- **Onboarding Wizard**: On your first login, launch the **Setup Wizard** from the top bar to configure your studio name, colors, showreel, and WhatsApp booking number in 6 guided steps.

---

## 2. Managing Video Projects & Multi-Orientation Cuts

Every project in your portfolio supports multiple video formats and aspect ratios:

### A. Aspect Ratios & Orientations
- **16:9 Landscape**: Standard cinematic commercial cuts, music videos, and widescreen film releases.
- **9:16 Vertical**: Native portrait orientation optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
- **1:1 Square**: Feed-ready square cuts.
- **2.35:1 Anamorphic**: Cinematic widescreen letterbox.

### B. Video Hosting Providers
- **YouTube**: Paste standard or unlisted YouTube links (e.g., `https://youtu.be/...`).
- **Vimeo**: Paste Vimeo showcase or review links.
- **Google Drive**: Browse and attach 4K Master MP4 files directly from your Google Drive without re-uploading.
- **Direct MP4 / CDN**: Paste direct cloud video file URLs.

### C. Before & After Color Grading Slider
- Enable the **Before / After Comparison Slider** on any project.
- Provide a link to your **RAW Log** frame and your **Final Film Graded** frame.
- Visitors can drag the divider left and right on desktop and mobile touchscreens to inspect your color science and LUT/emulation craftsmanship.

---

## 3. Google Drive Integration

1. In the Admin Dashboard, click **Drive Files** or **Pick From Google Drive** inside any project editor.
2. Browse your Google Drive folders (`Commercials`, `Music Videos`, `Social 9:16`, `Color Grading`).
3. Click **"Use in Master Cut"** or **"Use in 9:16 Reel"** to attach the file with zero server storage overhead.

## 4. Brand Studio & Aesthetic Customization

The **Brand Studio** tab lets you customize the entire visual identity:
- **Heading & Body Fonts**: Choose between Syne, Cinzel Decorative, Space Grotesk, Playfair Display, Outfit, and Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Color Tokens**: Customize Primary Accent (glow), Secondary Accent, Background, and Surface colors, or select a 1-click preset (Cyber Film, Neon Acid, VHS Ultraviolet, Golden 35mm, Monochrome Cinema).
- **Retro Texture Engine**:
  - Procedural Canvas 35mm Film Grain (Low / Medium / High)
  - CRT Scanlines & Screen Glitch
  - CRT Phosphor Glass Glow
  - VHS Tape Glitch / Tracking Noise
  - Live Viewfinder REC Indicator and Timecode HUD
- **Window Frame Styles**: Choose between `Retro Win95`, `Y2K Silver`, `CRT Terminal`, `VHS Player`, or `Minimal Frameless`.
- **Default Archive Layout**: Select `Surreal Collage`, `Standard Grid`, `9:16 Social Reels`, or `Contact Sheet Index`.

## 5. Client Inquiries, Bookings & WhatsApp

- **Inquiries Inbox**: When visitors submit project briefs via the booking form, transmissions are stored securely in your dashboard inbox with client budgets, timelines, footage links, and vision details.
- **WhatsApp Fast-Track**: Enable WhatsApp Direct in **Booking Form Settings** to allow clients to tap and chat directly with you for urgent commercial holds and fast quotes.
- **Formspree Support**: Optionally connect your Formspree endpoint (`https://formspree.io/f/xyz...`) for instant email forwarding.
